# 생명 시계 연구실 · Suno 음악 프롬프트

두 곡 모두 가사 없는 연주곡으로 제작한다. 메인은 탐험 중 반복해서 들어도 편안한 곡, 엔딩은 발견의 기쁨을 정리하는 곡이다. 같은 네 음의 상승 선율을 공유하는 방향이다. 선율의 실제 일치는 생성 결과를 듣고 확인한다.

## 메인 테마 · 숲의 시간이 시작되는 곳

```text
Instrumental theme for a cozy storybook forest exploration game. Curious, inviting, gently magical, full of discovery. Warm acoustic guitar, delicate marimba, playful pizzicato strings, airy flute and a few sparkling celesta notes. D major, 92 BPM, light 4/4 rhythm. Introduce a memorable four-note rising melody that feels like a tiny seed beginning to grow. Beautiful organic acoustic sound, polished game soundtrack, charming without sounding like a nursery rhyme. Gentle momentum, restrained percussion, consistent soft dynamics, space for dialogue and sound effects. Start with the melody immediately. Around two minutes, loop-friendly arrangement with a subtle ending that reconnects naturally to the opening. No vocals, no choir, no heavy drums, no EDM, no dramatic cinematic hits.
```

## 파이널 엔딩 테마 · 우리가 깨운 숲

```text
Instrumental ending theme for a heartfelt storybook forest adventure. The forest is alive again after a young explorer discovers the secrets of butterflies, seeds and changing seasons. Warm, hopeful, quietly triumphant, with the feeling of “I discovered this myself.” D major, 80 BPM, gentle 4/4. Reprise a simple four-note rising melody, first on intimate felt piano and celesta, then joined by acoustic guitar, lyrical flute and warm chamber strings. Gradually blossom into a luminous, emotionally satisfying finale, tender rather than epic or sentimental. Around two minutes. Finish with a clear resolved ending: strings soften, piano repeats the little melody, one delicate bell note, then a long gentle decay. No vocals, no choir, no heavy percussion, no sad ending, no trailer-style climax.
```

메인은 짧은 시작부와 자연스러운 반복 연결, 엔딩은 마지막 여운과 끝맺음을 듣고 선택한다. 완성 파일을 받은 뒤 게임에 적용한다.
